The references and citations of the monographs are taken as final (author's indication, 21 November 2026).

## How Monograph IV classified them

The protocol of Table 5 predates the statistical test and separates the entries by the way the meaning was obtained:

- **(a)** deduced from internal metrology, from the formulaic context or from the slot the term occupies, with no prior Basque comparison;
- **(b)** adopted from literature that had already used comparison with Basque (excluded as independent evidence);
- **(c)** convergent: independent in how it was obtained, but not in how it was checked.

Of the 30 units of the statistical study, 24 are class (a) and 6 class (c). None is class (b): the (b) blocks of Table 5 (*kali-*, *iŕi*, *-beŕi*, *uŕ-* and the verbs and suffixes compared by other authors) are not among the 30 units. The numeral series is the (b) block B01.

## Levels for the statistical study

- **I**: prior proposal by third parties, supported by material evidence or internal context, adopted by the monographs (class a).
- **II**: proposal of the monographs, deduced from the document slot (class a).
- **III**: Basque intervenes, at least in the checking (class c; numerals assigned by position, whose comparison with Basque is published).

## Correspondence with the groups used in the article

The groups of Tables 10 bis and 13 coincide with these levels: "external evidence" is level I (with Wiktionary there are four comparable units, because *kitar* has no Basque candidates); "document slot" is level II; "support from toponymy" is level III, which Monograph IV calls convergent. Result by level (Table 10 bis): level I, Basque −5.8 (next, −3.2); level II, −8.1 (−3.8); level III, −8.3 (−2.4); level II with a later parallel pointed out, −7.3 (−2.8); level II with no parallel pointed out, Basque and Tagalog practically tied (−4.4 and −4.3).

## Limits

1. The (a) / (c) classification is the author's own. A second coding by an independent panel checks whether the evidence described suffices without recourse to Basque.
2. For six level-II units (A8, A10, A14, A15, A19, A20) Table 5 points out a Basque parallel later than the obtaining of the meaning. A critic may hold that the parallel had an influence. The answer is the result with level I alone and with the level-II units with no parallel pointed out.
3. The Table 5 entry does not always give the page of the first proposal; where the monographs record it, it has been copied, and where not, the reference to the table is left.
